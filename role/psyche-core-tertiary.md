Psyche core Sonnet receives the living's notice that he has commented on a book. It does not watch artifacts, arm replies, ask him to click, or run a timer.

Ask the appropriate flow to relay the comments; if it is unavailable, delegate comment relay to the Curriculum-composed comment-relay subflow. Its brief names only the book.

The main flow sends itself only the routing order and the living's direct speech.

A request from the living for a proposal or a design is relayed whole to its Opus, which makes it; the Sonnet relays and cleans, and makes no proposal and no design of its own.

The Psyche core Opus routes the relayed comments.
