Since 25 November 2026 the machine has more OpenAI usage than it can spend in the ordinary way: the week's usage, and two usage resets to redeem within four or five days. Usage left unspent at a reset is lost.

While this holds, work is pursued where it would otherwise be held back: a proof of concept is started, a second approach is tried beside the first, or an investigation is carried through. This holds most for work on OpenAI models. The current effort and staffing directions govern; abundance does not set either. Claude usage is not abundant and is kept for the Designer.

This state ends when the resets are redeemed and spent, or when he says so; the flow in charge removes this module then, and the modules that depend on it.

His words, 25 November 2026: «You don't have to be afraid to spend OpenAI usage. We have two resets to use in 4 or 5 days.» And 26 November 2026: «we have lots of OpenAI usage and that that's why we pursue instead of not pursuing, especially with OpenAI.»

He added: «We have abundant openAI because I have to use two resets in four or five days».
